About us

Two people. One obsession with getting you noticed.

OJ Marketing is Olivia and Jackson. We started it in Orem because we kept seeing great local businesses that nobody knew about. We think that's fixable.

Jackson McBee

Co-Founder

Jackson believes good marketing comes down to one question: is this bringing in customers? He co-founded OJ Marketing to help Utah County businesses get a clear answer, through paid media on Google and Meta, social media marketing, and videography.

He studies marketing at Utah Valley University. He previously worked in sales at The Green House, UVU's student-run marketing agency, where he worked directly with local business owners on their growth goals. He has also sold home security door to door for Brinks Home, so he knows what it takes to win over someone who wasn't expecting a knock.

Jackson also spent two years serving in Luanda, Angola, where he became fluent in Portuguese. He learned there that you earn people's trust by listening first.

When he's not working, Jackson is usually on the driving range trying to fix his swing, reading, or losing a board game to his wife.

Olivia Carvalho

Co-Founder

Olivia Carvalho is an entrepreneur and marketer who helps businesses grow through smart marketing, strong sales, and great customer experiences. She graduated from Utah Valley University with a Bachelor’s in Marketing, a minor in Enterprise and Development, and a Certificate in Sales.

Olivia has launched and grown service businesses from the ground up. That hands-on work gave her real experience in leadership, business development, customer acquisition, and operations management. She is happiest spotting opportunities, solving problems, and turning ideas into measurable results, and she works best where strategy, hard work, and creativity come together.

Olivia takes initiative and moves quickly, without losing sight of the customer or the team around her. She believes great businesses are built on trust, strong relationships, and delivering value every time. She brings that standard to every project and is always glad to connect with people who share her passion for entrepreneurship, marketing, and building businesses that last.
