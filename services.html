Services

Three ways we create buzz.

Each one works on its own, and they work even better together. Here's what each one is, in plain English, and what it actually does for a business.

01

Social media marketing

We run your Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok so you don't have to. That means planning what to post, creating it, posting it consistently, and talking with the people who comment and message you.

What it does for you

  • Keeps you top of mind, so when someone needs what you sell, they think of you first
  • Builds trust. People check your page before they visit or buy
  • Turns happy customers into free word-of-mouth when they share and tag you
7 in 10 U.S. adults use Facebook, and half use Instagram. Source: Pew Research Center, 2025
02

Paid media

Paid media means ads: on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Google. Instead of hoping the right people find you, you pay to show up in front of exactly who you want, like people within 10 miles who are into what you offer.

What it does for you

  • Gets results fast. Ads can start bringing in traffic the same day they go live
  • Targets the right people by location, age, and interests, so money isn't wasted
  • Easy to measure. You see exactly what you spent and what came back
81% of consumers say social media drives them to make impulse purchases. Source: Sprout Social Index, 2025
03

Videography & content creation

We come to you and film. Short videos for Reels and TikTok, behind-the-scenes clips, customer stories, and photos of your space and products. Then we edit it all so it's ready to post.

What it does for you

  • Video is what the apps push the most, so it reaches more people than a plain photo
  • Shows people what it's like to be your customer before they walk in
  • One shoot gives you weeks of posts and material for ads
91% of businesses now use video as a marketing tool. Source: Wyzowl Video Marketing Report, 2026
