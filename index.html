Questions

Good to know: working with an Orem marketing agency.

What does OJ Marketing do?

OJ Marketing is a marketing agency in Orem, Utah. We run social media for local businesses, manage Google and Meta ads, and film and edit video and photo content. Every client starts with a free marketing audit.

How much does a marketing agency cost in Utah County?

Our social media management and our ads management are each $1,000 a month before discounts. New clients get 10% off, adding a second service takes 10% off, and 6 or 12 month terms save 15 to 20%. Video is priced per project.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. You can go month to month. 3, 6 and 12 month terms are optional, and 6 and 12 month terms come with a discount. See pricing for the details.

Which cities do you work in?

We're based in Orem and work with businesses in Orem, Provo, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Vineyard, Springville, Spanish Fork, Saratoga Springs and the rest of Utah County. We come to you for shoots and meetings.

Can you work with me in Portuguese?

Yes. Jackson speaks Portuguese fluently and can help you in Portuguese from the first call. Just call or text.

How soon will I see results?

Ads usually start bringing in useful data in the first week, and we adjust them every week from there. Social media takes longer: plan on about 2 to 3 months of consistent posting before it really builds.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your social media and ad accounts, about 30 minutes for the free audit, and photos or a filming day if you want video content.

Do you offer SEO?

Not yet. Right now we focus on social media, Google and Meta ads, and video. If search is your biggest gap, we'll tell you in your free audit.
