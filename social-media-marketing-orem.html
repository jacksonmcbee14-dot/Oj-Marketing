What it is

Social media marketing means we plan what to post, create it, post it consistently, and talk with the people who comment and message you. You stay focused on running your business while your pages stay active and on-brand.

What's included

  • A content plan built around your goals
  • Posts designed, written and scheduled
  • Comments and DMs handled
  • Monthly results report
  • Videos and photos from our shoots, when you add video

What it does for your business

Most of your future customers already scroll Instagram, Facebook or TikTok every day. Consistent, good-looking posts make sure they see you there, recognize you, and think of you when they need what you sell.

Why it works

  • Keeps you top of mind, so when someone needs what you sell, they think of you first
  • Builds trust. People check your page before they visit or buy
  • Turns happy customers into free word-of-mouth when they share and tag you
